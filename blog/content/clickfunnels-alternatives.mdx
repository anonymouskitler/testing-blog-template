The best ClickFunnels alternative depends on what you outgrew. Systeme.io suits small budgets, GoHighLevel suits agencies, Kartra suits course sellers, Leadpages suits single landing pages, and AI funnel builders suit teams that want to launch from a written prompt. Compare on total cost, funnel depth and fit.

## Key takeaways

- ClickFunnels has no free plan. It runs from $97 to $297 a month, plus a $5,997-a-year Dominate tier, and the price climbs with contacts and emails.
- A landing page builder and a funnel builder are different tools. Pick the one that matches how many steps your buyer takes.
- Compare advertised price against real cost: usage billing, contact caps and transaction fees change the answer.
- Run the same offer through a trial of two finalists before you migrate anything.

## What does a ClickFunnels alternative need to do?

A ClickFunnels alternative has to do the same core job: take a visitor through linked pages (opt-in, offer, checkout, upsell, thank-you) and collect payment and contact details along the way. If a tool only builds single pages, it replaces part of ClickFunnels, not all of it.

That split explains most of the confusion in search results. [Landing page builders such as Leadpages, Unbounce and Instapage create standalone pages; funnel builders such as Systeme.io, ClickFunnels, GoHighLevel and Kartra link several pages into a guided journey with email and payments built in](https://systemebuilder.com/best-funnel-builders/).

## Why do people look for alternatives?

The usual reasons are price, contact-based billing and fit. ClickFunnels is billed [at $97 (Launch), $197 (Scale) and $297 (Optimize) a month, plus an annual-only Dominate plan at $5,997 a year](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/clickfunnels-pricing/). The higher tiers mostly raise contact and email limits, so a growing list raises the bill.

Plans were restructured in June 2025, so older comparison articles often quote retired prices. [ClickFunnels' own comparison page lists the legacy Basic, Pro and FunnelHacker plans as retired](https://support.myclickfunnels.com/docs/comparing-legacy-and-current-clickfunnels-pricing-plans/). Check the [current pricing page](https://www.clickfunnels.com/pricing) before you compare.

### Systeme.io: the budget pick

Systeme.io is the cheapest full funnel builder in most comparisons. [Its free plan includes 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course and unlimited email sends with no transaction fees, and paid tiers run $17, $47 and $97 a month](https://www.aicofounderstack.com/2026/09/08/clickfunnels-vs-kartra-vs-systeme-io-2026-founder-guide/). Choose it when you are validating an offer.

### GoHighLevel: the agency pick

GoHighLevel combines funnels with a CRM, two-way SMS, booking and the ability to white-label the platform for clients. Budget for usage, though. [One review reports real bills of $400 to $600 a month once email, SMS and phone are billed as usage](https://realcostlabs.com/best-all-in-one-marketing-platform/).

### Kartra: the course-seller pick

Kartra bundles landing pages, email, membership sites, video hosting and affiliate management. [Its entry Essentials plan takes a 5% transaction fee and caps contacts at 500](https://www.aicofounderstack.com/2026/09/08/clickfunnels-vs-kartra-vs-systeme-io-2026-founder-guide/), so most sellers look at Starter and above.

### Leadpages: the landing-page pick

Leadpages is fast and its price does not rise with your contact count, but [it is not a complete suite and usually needs a separate email and CRM tool](https://newbluesky.online/2026/09/28/clickfunnels-vs-systeme-io-gohighlevel-kartra-leadpages-the-complete-2026-comparison/). Choose it when one page and one offer is the whole job.

### AI funnel builders: the fast-draft pick

A newer group of tools, including [Prompt Funnels](https://promptfunnels.com/), [FlexiFunnels](https://flexifunnels.com/flexi) and [Funnelloops](https://funnelloops.com/), generate a funnel's pages, copy and automations from a plain-English description. They suit founders who want a working first draft in minutes. Because the category is new, test integrations, limits and export options on a trial before you commit.

## How should you choose?

Choose by stage and workload, not by feature count. Work through these steps:

1. **Write down your funnel.** Count the steps from first click to payment, including upsells.
2. **Add up real cost.** Include contacts, emails, SMS, transaction fees and extra seats at the list size you expect in 12 months.
3. **Check what you must keep.** Payment processor, email tool, domain and course content all need a migration path.
4. **Trial two finalists.** Rebuild your best-performing funnel in each and compare setup time and conversion.
5. **Decide on evidence.** Move only when the alternative matches your current funnel's results.

ClickFunnels still earns its price in some cases. [Reviewers describe it as strongest for established, funnel-first sellers with proven offers who use its templates and upsell tooling](https://www.aicofounderstack.com/2026/09/08/clickfunnels-vs-kartra-vs-systeme-io-2026-founder-guide/). If that is you, staying is a valid result of the comparison.

## FAQ

### What is the best free ClickFunnels alternative?

Systeme.io is the most commonly recommended free option. Its free plan covers funnels, email and a course for up to 2,000 contacts, per the review sources above.

### Is ClickFunnels worth the money?

It is worth it if funnels generate your revenue and you want one platform for pages, payments, email and courses. If you only need simple landing pages, cheaper tools do that job.

### What is the difference between a funnel builder and a landing page builder?

A landing page builder makes single pages for one offer or campaign. A funnel builder links multiple pages, payments and email into one guided journey.

### Can I move my funnels from ClickFunnels to another tool?

You will usually rebuild pages in the new tool, since layouts are not portable between platforms. Plan time to reconnect your domain, payment processor and email list, and keep the old account until the new funnel converts.

### Do AI funnel builders replace ClickFunnels?

They can for simple and mid-complexity funnels, and they shorten the first draft. For complex multi-step funnels with heavy upsell logic, confirm that the tool supports what you run today.
